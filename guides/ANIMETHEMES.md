# Openings and endings

Every anime's page, and every series' page, ends with its openings and endings:
the song, who sings it, which episodes it plays in, and a play button for the
video or just the sound. They also power **Guess the Opening/Ending** on the
Games page, where you hear a short clip and name the anime.

There is nothing to set up. It works without a key or an account.

## Where it comes from

The songs, artists and videos come from [AnimeThemes](https://animethemes.moe/),
a community-run site. The app doesn't copy any video or audio: it keeps the list
of songs and where AnimeThemes hosts them, refreshes that list once a week, and
plays the files from AnimeThemes when you ask for one. A new show's songs appear
after the next weekly refresh. On a fresh install the first read starts as soon as
the app does and takes several minutes; until it has finished, the pages say the
songs aren't here yet.

Some themes are marked as NSFW or as containing spoilers. They stay in the list
with a mark. A spoiler's video waits until you ask for it, and with **Hide NSFW**
on, so does an NSFW theme's. The game never plays a spoiler, and with
**Hide NSFW** on it never plays an NSFW theme either.

## Guess the Opening/Ending

You hear 0.1 seconds of a song, then 0.5, 1, 3, 5 and 10, and each wrong guess or
skip gives you the next length. The game keeps no score. When you get a song
(or run out of lengths) it shows the anime and plays the video, and **Next song**
moves on. On the setup screen you choose openings, endings or both, along with
the same year, type and list settings as the other games.

If the sound doesn't start, press the Play button: browsers only allow sound after
a press, and the game says so when it needs one.

## Terms

AnimeThemes' terms allow non-commercial use, which is what a copy of this app on
your own computer is. **If you run a fork commercially, or host it for other
people, get AnimeThemes' approval first.** Their terms and contact are on their
site.

The app credits AnimeThemes under **Credits** in Settings. It isn't affiliated
with AnimeThemes.
